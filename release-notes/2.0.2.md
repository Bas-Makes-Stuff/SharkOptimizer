# SharkOptimizer 2.0.2

- Five primary sidebar entries: Home, Optimize, Games, Tools, and Settings.
- Grouped optimization previews with Show all views for FPS and latency, network, privacy, Windows settings, and advanced options.
- Compact tweak cards with details on demand, risk labels, and thin green borders for enabled settings.
- Separate General and Presets views; detected external settings remain distinct from tracked undo history.
- Game Boost is under Games, with focused Session, Profile, Apps, and History views.
- A Tools overview and separate utility/cleanup views replace long pages of unrelated controls.
- New navigation labels translated into all supported languages.
